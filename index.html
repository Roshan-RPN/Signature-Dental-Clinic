Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our dental services

Is root canal treatment painful at Maruthi Dental Clinic?

Not at all. We use gentle, advanced anaesthesia and modern endodontic techniques to ensure a completely pain-free experience. Our patients consistently report feeling little to no pain during root canals.

How long does invisible aligner treatment take?

Treatment duration varies based on individual needs, but most aligner treatments take between 6 to 18 months. During your consultation, Dr. Dhana Bharath will explain your personalized treatment timeline and budget.

Do you offer dental care for children?

Yes, we specialize in pediatric dentistry. Dr. Karunya is exceptionally kind and calm with children, making them feel highly comfortable and completely at ease during their dental procedures.

What safety and sterilisation measures do you follow?

We follow rigorous clinical sterilisation protocols with autoclaving of all instruments, use of disposable materials, and thorough surface disinfection between appointments. Patient safety is our highest priority.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by calling us directly at +91 94487 03632, sending a WhatsApp message, or filling out the contact form. Taking prior appointments is highly recommended to avoid any waiting times.

Do you offer root canals and crowns at affordable prices?

Yes, we are known for having the most competitive and honest prices in Whitefield. We offer top-grade materials, such as Zirconia crowns at highly affordable rates (starting at just ₹6,000) with flexible payment plans.
